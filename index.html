What is Temporary Email?

Temporary email is a disposable email address that can be used for short-term registrations and online services. It helps keep your personal email address private and reduces unwanted messages.


This page provides the frontend interface of a temporary mailbox. A backend SMTP server is required to receive real emails from the Internet.

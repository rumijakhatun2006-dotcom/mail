What is Multi Mail Disposable Temporary Email?

Multi Mail is a free, secure, and instant temporary email service powered by the robust Mail.tm API. It allows you to create throwaway email addresses to receive confirmation messages, activation links, or test emails without disclosing your actual email address. Protecting your primary address prevents spam, targeted ads, and malicious data harvesting.

100% Anonymous

No registration, phone numbers, or personal identifying data required to generate throwaway mailboxes.

Instant Delivery

Auto-refresh polling checks the inbox instantly as soon as messages arrive on the Mail.tm server.

Auto Destruction

Messages automatically delete after a brief period, ensuring no persistent digital footprint.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Multi Mail free to use?

Yes, Multi Mail is completely free with no hidden fees or premium subscriptions.

Can I send emails from this temporary address?

To prevent abuse and spam, sending emails is disabled. Multi Mail is strictly designed for receiving emails.

How long does my temporary email address last?

Your temporary address remains active as long as your browser session is preserved or until you click "New" to generate a fresh address.

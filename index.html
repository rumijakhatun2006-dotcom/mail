What is Multi Mail Disposable Email?

Multi Mail is a free temporary email service powered by secure, high-speed API endpoints. Disposable email addresses are short-term, throwaway mailboxes that allow you to receive emails, activate accounts, and download gated assets without revealing your actual personal email address.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Multi Mail completely free to use?

Yes, Multi Mail is 100% free with no registration or payment required.

How long does a temporary email address last?

Your email address remains active during your active session. Email messages and accounts automatically purge from API servers after 24 hours.

Can I send emails from Multi Mail?

No, Multi Mail is designed exclusively for receiving emails to maintain strict anti-spam policy compliance.
